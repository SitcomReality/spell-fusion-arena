	circle_01 - circle_05.png are various different styles of circle textures for use as sprites. These are mostly circle 'outlines' of varying 'glows' and 'shadows'.
	
	fire_01 & fire_02.png are kind of like fire 'clouds'.
	
	flare_01.png is a horizontal lens flare-type texture
	
	light_01 - light_03.png are vaguely assymetrical circles with more inner fill than the circle_0n pngs.
	
	magic_01 - pentagram with circles at each vertex
	magic_02 - octagon with circles at each vertex
	magic_03 - circle with 4 points
	magic_04 - 4 points with small circle
	magic_05 - a 4-pointed star

	scorch_01.png, scorch_02.png, scorch_03.png - "Scorch"-type texture depicts 3 stages going from a spikey explosion to a cloudy puff
	
	scratch_01.png depicts 3 paralellel scratch lines on a diagonal from bottom left to top right. This sprite should be used as a particle that appears and disappears in place, without translating its position at all.
	
	slash_01.png depicts a semi-circle pointing down. This sprite should be rotated to point the direction that it moves in.
	
	smoke_01 & smoke_02.png - clouds of smoke. These have less definition than fire.
	
	spark_01 - spark_04.png - assymetrical zaps of electricity that would work as an animated sequence
	
	star_01 - star_09.png - various star, lens-flare and spikey circle textures
	
	symbol_01.png - loveheart
	symbol_02.png - 5-pointed star symbol/icon
	
	trace_01 - trace_05.png - a tall vertical streak, in a sequence where it dissipates
	trace_01 - straight and narrow streak
	trace_02 - slightly wobbly streak
	trace_03 - very wavy
	trace_04 - streak dissipating into smoke with energy concentrated at the bottom
	trace_05 - a bright patch in the smokey vertical cloud streak is towards the top of the projectile
	
	trace_06 - a thick glowing vertical line 
	

Apart from slash_01 (which will always point in the direction of its movement vector) and trace_01 - trace_09, all sprites should be rotated by a random amount when they spawn so they don't all look uniform. The magic_01 & magic_02 sprites should also rotate during their lifetime to make them look more magical.
